01 / About

Security is a mindset, not a checklist.

I'm a cybersecurity professional with 4+ years across vulnerability assessment, penetration testing, configuration reviews and client consulting. I hold CRTP, CRTA, C|EH v11 and Certified Cyber Criminologist credentials, with a focus on finding real risk and turning it into practical remediation.
